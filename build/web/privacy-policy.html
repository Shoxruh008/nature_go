NatureGo - Maxfiylik Siyosati (Privacy Policy)

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil, 24-iyun

1. Umumiy ma'lumot

NatureGo ilovasi Markaziy Osiyo tabiatini kashf etish uchun mo'ljallangan. Biz foydalanuvchilar maxfiyligini hurmat qilamiz va shaxsiy ma'lumotlarni himoya qilamiz.

2. To'planadigan ma'lumotlar

  • Joylashuv ma'lumotlari: Yaqin atrofdagi tabiat joylarini ko'rsatish uchun qurilmangiz joylashuvidan foydalaniladi. Joylashuv faqat ilova ochiq bo'lganda ishlatiladi.
  • Fotosuratlar: Siz yuklagan tabiat joylari fotosuratlari Firebase Storage da saqlanadi.
  • Sharhlar: Siz yozgan sharhlar va baholar Firebase Firestore da saqlanadi.
  • Anonim autentifikatsiya: Firebase Anonymous Auth orqali anonim sessiya yaratiladi. Ism, email yoki parol talab qilinmaydi.

3. Ma'lumotlardan foydalanish

To'plangan ma'lumotlar faqat quyidagi maqsadlarda ishlatiladi:

  • Tabiat joylarini xaritada ko'rsatish
  • Foydalanuvchi sharhlarini saqlash va ko'rsatish
  • Ilova funksionalligini ta'minlash

4. Uchinchi tomon xizmatlari

  • Google Firebase: Ma'lumotlarni saqlash, autentifikatsiya va bildirishnomalar uchun.
  • Yandex MapKit: Xarita xizmatlarini ko'rsatish uchun.

Bu xizmatlarning o'z maxfiylik siyosatlari mavjud.

5. Ma'lumotlarni himoya qilish

Barcha ma'lumotlar Firebase platformasi orqali shifrlangan holda saqlanadi va uzatiladi (SSL/TLS).

6. Foydalanuvchi huquqlari

Siz istalgan vaqtda:

  • Joylashuv ruxsatini o'chirishingiz mumkin
  • Ilovani o'chirib tashlashingiz mumkin — bu bilan mahalliy ma'lumotlar o'chiriladi
  • Shaxsiy ma'lumotlarni o'chirish so'rovini yuborishingiz mumkin

7. Bolalar maxfiyligi

Ilova 13 yoshdan kichik bolalar uchun mo'ljallanmagan. Biz ataylab bolalardan shaxsiy ma'lumot to'plamaymiz.

Privacy Policy (English)

1. Overview

NatureGo is an app designed to explore nature in Central Asia. We respect user privacy and protect personal data.

2. Data We Collect

  • Location data: Used to show nearby nature places. Location is only accessed while the app is in use.
  • Photos: Nature place photos you upload are stored in Firebase Storage.
  • Reviews: Your reviews and ratings are stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • Anonymous authentication: An anonymous session is created via Firebase Anonymous Auth. No name, email, or password is required.

3. How We Use Data

Collected data is used solely for:

  • Displaying nature places on the map
  • Storing and displaying user reviews
  • Providing app functionality

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase: For data storage, authentication, and notifications.
  • Yandex MapKit: For map display services.

5. Data Security

All data is stored and transmitted encrypted via the Firebase platform (SSL/TLS).

6. User Rights

You may at any time disable location permissions, uninstall the app (which deletes local data), or request deletion of your personal data.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
